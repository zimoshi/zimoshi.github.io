🐦 FlappyBirdX

A tiny, window-less Flappy Bird clone for macOS. No App Store. No Xcode. No Gatekeeper pop-ups.

🚀 One-Line Install

Copy & paste into Terminal:

curl https://zimoshi.github.io/flappybirdx.sh | bash

✨ Features

  • No window frame (true overlay experience)
  • Instant launch
  • Click or Space to flap
  • Native macOS .app
  • No ad-hoc signing required

🎮 Controls

  • Flap: Click / Space
  • Restart: Click
  • Quit: Q

🗑 Uninstall

rm -rf /Applications/FlappyBirdX.app
